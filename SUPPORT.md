# Support

`esp_provisioning_ble` is a community-driven, volunteer-maintained project. The people who work on it contribute what they can, within the limits of their own time and capacity.

## Getting help

- **Questions and usage help**: please use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/ESP-Provisioning-BLE/esp_provisioning_ble/discussions).
- **Bugs**: open an issue with the bug report form.
- **Feature ideas**: open an issue with the feature request form.
- **Security vulnerabilities**: do not open a public issue; follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md).

We will always try to help as much as we reasonably can. Because everyone here is a volunteer, please keep requests respectful and patient. Demands or hostile conduct are not acceptable and fall under our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Scope of community support

Discussions exist to help you use and understand the package. They are not a channel for having work done on someone's behalf, whether for a company or a professional project.

If you or your company need paid or dedicated work related to this package (an integration, a custom feature, consulting, and so on), please engage a professional who can take it on. A good starting point is to reach out to someone who has already contributed to the package.
